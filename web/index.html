How a warning gets out

The model proposes. Code, reviewers and people decide.

  1. SRC · CEvent inputsEach marked verified or not. The operator picks the zone.
  2. MDrafter and translatorAI writes CAP fields in English and Arabic. It has no send function.
  3. P12 rulesPlain code. A failed rule blocks; a missing approval holds.
  4. P4 reviewer agentsMet office, issuer, operator, Arabic. Each must quote a clause word for word.
  5. D2 named peopleRead open objections, approve, then the alert is signed.

    G1 · ITU-T SG17

    CAP has no field for AI involvement or a named approver.

    In 1,711 live alerts from 8 feeds, none records either. We propose five registered CAP parameters, signed with the alert. A draft profile is in the repository.

    Limits

    What this does not prove.

    Are the replays the original systems?

    No. Each one is rebuilt from public reports. Where the record is silent, we assume the alert was fine. Some incidents rest on official statements rather than investigation reports, and the India case on press reports only.

    Who wrote the faults and answer keys?

    The 30-fault set and its matching terms are ours. The 24 pre-registered faults were written by a separate AI agent that saw only the drafts, and committed before the run. They were not written by alerting professionals. The replays use the investigators' own findings as the answer key.

    Are the reviewer agents the real institutions?

    No. They stand in for roles. Two models play the six agents: one drafts and translates, another reviews.

    What about the UAE specifics?

    The issuer register, target zones and signing keys are demo stand-ins. TDRA's 2019 UAE-Alert specification is the primary text for handset behaviour; the 2015 handset rules come from a compliance firm's summary.

    What do the misses mean?

    R11 only checks AI-written text, so a wrong human template (Taiwan 2024) gets past the rules. The met office reviewer caught it. A grounding check should run on every alert. Every held-out result is shown as it came out, misses included.
